# 🚗 Road Traffic Deaths Dashboard — Saudi Arabia
### SDG Indicator 3.6.1 | Vision 2030 Progress Assessment

![Dashboard](dashboard-screenshot.png)

---

## 📌 Project Summary

In this project, I analyzed the road traffic death rate in Saudi Arabia for SDG Indicator 3.6.1. I started with a raw Excel file that only had 2 data points, did some research to find the missing numbers, and built an interactive Excel dashboard to show the actual progress.

---

## 📊 Key Results

| Indicator | Value | Source |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| SA National Rate (2024) | 12.13 per 100k | HST 2024 |
| WHO Global Average | 15.0 per 100k | WHO Report 2021 |
| Reduction since 2016 | 57.88% | HST 2024 |
| Vision 2030 Target | < 10 per 100k | UNECE/NTP |
| Lives saved per year | ~4,608 | Alharbi 2025 |

---

## 🔍 About the Data & Process

The sheet I started with only had 2 numbers. To make a proper dashboard, I manually searched for the historical data and cross-checked everything across 4 official sources:

- ① Health Sector Transformation Report 2024 (Vision 2030)
- ② UN Stats SDG Portal
- ③ WHO Global Status Report 2023
- ④ Alharbi, R.J. (2025) — *Scientific Reports / Nature*

---
